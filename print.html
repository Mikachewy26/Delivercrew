A SIMPLE PROCESS

Tell us the idea. We’ll help bring it to life.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Share your product, quantity, preferred size and required date. Let us know whether you need design, printing or both.

  2. Agree the details

    We’ll confirm the brief, print specification and quote. If you have artwork ready, we’ll check its suitability for the chosen print format.

  3. Review and approve

    Review your design proof and check the wording, contact details and layout. Printing proceeds once the final artwork and specification are approved.

  4. Print and deliver

    We’ll arrange production and delivery, or coordinate your leaflets with a distribution campaign in your chosen areas.

GOOD TO KNOW

Planning your design and print project

Can you help if I don’t have a design?

Yes. Send us a little about your business, your audience and the message you want to share. We can create new artwork or develop your existing ideas, with the design work and price agreed before we start.

What should I send for a print quote?

Tell us the product, size, quantity, whether you need single or double-sided printing, and your preferred completion date. Include your artwork if you have it. If you’re unsure about paper or finishes, we can discuss the options with you.

Can you print my existing artwork?

Yes. Send your artwork, ideally as a print-ready PDF. We’ll confirm the file requirements, including bleed and resolution, for your chosen product and let you know if adjustments are needed.

Can you distribute my flyers and leaflets too?

Yes. We can combine design and printing with GPS-tracked leaflet distribution across Brighton & Hove and Sussex. Choose your target areas and we’ll agree the distribution arrangements as part of your campaign.
