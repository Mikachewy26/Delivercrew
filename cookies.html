Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and similar technologies used on the DeliverCrew website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files or pieces of information that websites can store on your device. They can be used to make a website work, remember choices and understand how a website is used.

Essential website storage

Our website may use essential browser storage or similar technologies where needed to provide features such as retaining campaign choices during a secure checkout journey. These technologies are used to provide functionality requested by you.

Third-party services

Some features may connect to third-party services, including secure payment, mapping, hosting and website infrastructure providers. Those providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services and may have their own privacy or cookie policies.

Optional cookies

If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies that require consent, we will update this policy and provide an appropriate choice before those cookies are used where required by law.

Managing cookies

You can control or remove cookies using your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may prevent some website features from working correctly.

If you have questions about our use of cookies or similar technologies, please use our Contact us page.
